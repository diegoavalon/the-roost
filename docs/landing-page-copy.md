## Section — How it works

### Six weeks. A clear loop. Real measurement.

**Week 0 — Diagnostic.**
You take an official practice test so we know your true starting point and exactly which skills to target.

**Weeks 1–6 — Practice and live sessions.**
Each week you work original, printable practice sets built for the highest-yield skills. Scan the code on each sheet, enter your answers, and get them graded instantly — with an explanation for every question written to actually make sense. Then a weekly live session walks through the reasoning with the group.

**Track your trend.**
Your own dashboard shows what's improving and what still needs work, week over week. You see the progress as it happens.

**Week 6 — Final.**
Another official practice test. The difference between week 0 and week 6 is your foothold.
