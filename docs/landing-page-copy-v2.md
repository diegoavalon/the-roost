## Section — How It Works

You take an official practice test so we know your true starting point and exactly which skills to target.

**Weeks 1–6 — Practice and live sessions.**
Each week you work original, printable practice sets built for the highest-yield skills. Scan the code on each sheet, enter your answers, and get them graded instantly — with an explanation for every question written to actually make sense. **Track your trend.**
Your own dashboard shows what's improving and what still needs work, week over week. You see the progress as it happens.

**Week 6 — Final.**
Another official practice test. The difference between your diagnostic and your final is your foothold.
